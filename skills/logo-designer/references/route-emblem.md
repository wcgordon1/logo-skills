# Emblem route

Use an emblem when containment communicates belonging, authority, craft, heritage, or community. A modern emblem is a composed mark inside a boundary, not necessarily a vintage seal.

## Construction directions

- **Modern institutional:** restrained container, clear symbol, minimal type, confident spacing.
- **Craft/service badge:** one category cue, sturdy outline, useful on uniforms or vehicles.
- **Community crest:** shared symbol and stronger sense of membership without heraldic clutter.
- **App-ready medallion:** compact central form with a clean enclosing shape.

Use a circle, shield, lozenge, rounded square, or custom container only when its geometry contributes to the concept.

## Prompt scaffold

```text
Create an original modern emblem logo for [brand/category].
Purpose: [authority/craft/belonging/community]. Central idea: [symbol].
Container: [shape and reason]. Composition: clear hierarchy, generous spacing,
limited elements, bold outer silhouette, one-color viability, readable at small size.
Exclude: tiny seal text, date/location filler, heraldic clutter, esports aggression,
crypto-coin styling, excessive stars or laurels, and mockup presentation.
```

## Repair failures

- Looks vintage unintentionally: simplify the border, remove filler text, and open spacing.
- Looks like esports: remove sharp shields, aggressive eyes, and exaggerated outlines.
- Looks like a crypto coin: avoid metallic coin rims and dense circular type.
- Too dense: retain the container plus one central idea; delete secondary ornaments.
- Container is arbitrary: let the internal geometry modify or break the boundary meaningfully.
