# Optional 3D rendering

3D is a presentation treatment, not a concept route. Apply it only after the mark passes flat, one-color, and small-size checks. Always preserve a flat companion master.

## Treatment options

| Treatment | Signal | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Soft 3D | Friendly, polished, tactile | Toy-like softness and weak edges |
| Glass | Technical, luminous, premium | Low contrast and generic glass blobs |
| Faceted | Precise, engineered, energetic | Busy facets and harsh stock-CGI appearance |
| Metallic | Durable, elite, industrial | Illegible reflections and luxury cliche |
| Inflated | Playful, approachable, current | Balloon-like distortion of the silhouette |
| Enamel | Crafted, collectible, app-ready | Excess outlines and badge-like density |

## Rendering contract

- Keep the exact flat silhouette and internal topology.
- Use one dominant material and one light direction.
- Keep the camera nearly orthographic or front-facing unless perspective is intentional.
- Use a plain background and strong edge separation.
- Prevent bevels, shadows, and highlights from becoming new design elements.
- Do not add scenery, platforms, floating particles, or unrelated props.

## Prompt addition

Append this only after the concept prompt is stable:

```text
Render the approved mark as [treatment]. Preserve the exact silhouette,
negative space, proportions, and color relationships. Front-facing centered view,
single controlled key light, restrained depth, clean plain background, premium material.
No new symbols, no text changes, no scenery, no pedestal, no particles, no mockup.
Also retain the flat master as the primary logo.
```

## Failure repair

- Shape drift: edit from the approved flat image and state the silhouette as invariant.
- Over-rendering: reduce depth, bevel width, reflections, and light count.
- Weak small-size read: use the flat version at small sizes rather than forcing 3D.
- Generic app-icon look: preserve the brand-specific topology and reduce decorative material effects.
- No viable flat counterpart: reject the treatment and revise the underlying mark.
