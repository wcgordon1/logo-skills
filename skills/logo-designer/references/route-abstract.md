# Abstract symbol route

Use an abstract mark when the brand needs a scalable, ownable symbol and no literal object should dominate. Premium abstract marks rely on proportion and topology, not arbitrary complexity.

## Concept logic

Anchor the geometry in a relationship from the brief: connection, acceleration, orchestration, focus, exchange, protection, or another product truth. The meaning may be subtle, but the construction must be deliberate.

Good structural moves include:

- modular units forming an unexpected whole
- a distinctive cut, fold, aperture, or negative-space event
- controlled repetition with one purposeful exception
- a continuous path with recognizable rhythm
- two forces resolving into a stable silhouette

Do not use a generic gradient blob, orbit, spark, infinity loop, hexagon, or letter-shaped ribbon without a proprietary transformation.

## Prompt scaffold

```text
Create an original abstract logo symbol for [brand/category].
Strategic idea: [relationship or motion]. Brand signal: [traits].
Distinctive topology: [specific cut/fold/path/modular rule].
Construction: restrained geometry, balanced negative space, bold silhouette,
few components, readable at 16 px, flat one-color viability.
Exclude: generic blobs, swooshes, sparks, infinity symbols, stock SaaS marks,
decorative gradients as the concept, text, and resemblance to cited brands.
```

## Repair failures

- Generic AI blob: impose a precise construction rule and simplify the perimeter.
- Too close to a reference: change topology, axis, proportion, and negative-space logic.
- Meaningless complexity: remove components until one relation remains.
- Visually cold: soften proportion or rhythm without adding illustrative detail.
- Depends on gradient: prove the form in black before restoring color.
